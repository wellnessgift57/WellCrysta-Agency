How to Show Up on Google Maps: A Simple Google Visibility Guide for Local Businesses

When customers search "near me", does your business appear, or does your competitor? Here are 7 practical steps to get found.

By WellCrysta  |  6 min read

Picture this. Someone in Pimpri-Chinchwad needs a service you offer, so they pick up their phone and search on Google. In a few seconds they see three businesses on the map, read a few reviews, tap one and call.

If you aren't one of those three, that customer never knew you existed, however good your work is.

This is why Google visibility matters. It means how easily people find your business when they search on Google Search and Google Maps. The good news is that you don't need a big budget to improve it. You need the right basics, done properly and consistently.

How Google Decides Which Businesses to Show

Google says local results are mainly based on three things: relevance, distance and prominence.

FactorWhat it meansCan you influence it?
RelevanceHow well your profile matches what the person searched forYes. Complete and accurate information helps
DistanceHow far your business is from the searcherNo. This is fixed by location
ProminenceHow well-known your business is, including reviews, ratings and mentions onlineYes. Build it steadily over time

Google is also clear on one point: there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Anyone who "guarantees" you the #1 spot on Maps is overpromising. What you can do is give Google accurate information and give customers good reasons to choose you.

The seven steps below focus on the parts you control.

Step 1: Claim and Verify Your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the free listing that appears on Google Search and Maps with your name, address, phone number, hours, photos and reviews. Think of it as your digital shopfront.

  • Search for your business on Google or Maps. If a listing already exists, claim it. Otherwise, create one.
  • Complete verification. Google says verifying tells it you're authorised to represent the business, which makes it more likely to show in results.

Step 2: Fill In Every Detail, Accurately

Google notes that businesses with complete and accurate information are more likely to appear in local results. Check that you have added:

  • Your full address (if customers can visit you)
  • Opening hours, including special hours for festivals and holidays
  • Phone number and website link
  • A clear business description in simple words
  • Extra details such as parking, accessibility or payment options
Tip: Use the same business name, address and phone number everywhere: your profile, website, Instagram and other listings. Conflicting details confuse both Google and customers.

Step 3: Choose the Right Categories and Services

Your category tells Google what kind of business you are. Choose the most specific primary category that matches what you do, then add secondary categories only where they truly apply. Add your services with short, clear descriptions, because this helps Google match you to what people actually search for.

Step 4: Add Real Photos and Videos

Photos show customers what to expect before they visit. Google recommends adding photos and videos to tell the story of your business. Upload:

  • Your shopfront, so people can recognise it when they arrive
  • The interior, your team and your work
  • Your products or services in use

Use your own, current images instead of stock photos. Real photos build trust.

Step 5: Collect and Reply to Reviews

Reviews and ratings are part of how Google judges prominence, and they strongly affect whether a customer picks you.

  • Ask happy customers right after a good experience. Share your review link by WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Reply to every review, positive or negative. Google notes that replying shows you value feedback.
  • Never buy or fake reviews. It breaks Google's policies and can get your profile penalised.

A steady flow of genuine reviews works better than a rush of reviews followed by silence.

Step 6: Make Your Website Fast, Clear and Easy to Contact

Your Google Business Profile gets people to your door. Your website helps convince them to walk in or call. Make sure it:

  • Loads quickly on mobile, since most local searches happen on phones
  • Clearly states what you do and where, for example "Dental clinic in Chinchwad, Pune"
  • Has a one-tap call or WhatsApp button
  • Shows your address, hours and map location
  • Uses a clear title and description for each page, so Google understands it

Step 7: Keep Your Profile Active

A profile that is updated regularly looks alive to customers. Make it a weekly habit to:

  • Post updates, offers or news on your profile
  • Add new photos
  • Update hours before festivals and holidays
  • Reply to new reviews and messages promptly
  • Check your profile's performance insights to see calls, direction requests and website clicks

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay Google to rank higher on Google Maps?

No. Google states there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Ads are separate from local ranking.

Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing your profile is free.

How long does it take to see results?

Profile corrections can show up within days. Gains from reviews, fresh content and website improvements usually build over weeks and months, so consistency matters more than a one-time effort.

Do I need a website to show up on Google?

A profile can appear without one, but a fast, clear website builds trust, makes it easy to contact you and gives Google more information about your business.
