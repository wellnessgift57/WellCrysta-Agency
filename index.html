The real problem

Strong growth starts with a strong foundation.

When growth feels stuck, three hidden gaps are usually the reason.

Slow, confusing pages

Most customers search on mobile. A slow or unclear page loses them before they ever get in touch.

Enquiries that leak

No WhatsApp button, a long form, or a slow reply. Customers message several businesses at once — the first helpful reply usually wins.

No idea what works

Without tracking, you cannot tell which effort brought a customer. Decisions become guesses and budgets get wasted.

FAQs

Questions business owners ask us

Why start with a foundation instead of running ads straight away?

Ads only send people to you. If the website is slow, the Google profile is incomplete, or enquiries are not tracked, ad money leaks away. The foundation makes sure every visitor can find you, trust you and reach you — and that every result is counted.

Who owns the website, domain and accounts?

You do. Domain, hosting, Google and Meta accounts are set up in your business name. If you ever move on, everything stays with you.

How long does the Foundation Setup take?

About 1.5 to 2 weeks (12–15 days). Work runs in parallel. The timeline assumes we receive your logo, photos, business details and account access on Day 1. Google profile verification is completed by you, and Google sets its own pace.

How much does it cost?

Every business starts from a different point, so we share a clear, fixed quote after your free consultation and audit — no hidden costs, and domain and hosting are billed at actual provider cost.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, business and team photos, profile details, access to Google and Meta accounts if you already have them, and approval of the final content.

Do you guarantee a number of leads or a #1 ranking?

No honest agency can guarantee rankings or lead numbers. What we do commit to are clear, verifiable handover checks — and transparent reporting on cost per enquiry once ads begin in Stage 2.

Which areas do you serve?

We work with businesses across India.
